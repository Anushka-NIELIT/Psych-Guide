Career Pathways: India vs Abroad

In India: Clinical Psychologist (RCI-licensed) → BA (Hons) → MA Clinical Psychology (RCI-approved) → 1–2 years internship. 6–7 year path. Counselling, Organizational/HR, Research, Social Sector also viable.

Abroad (UK/US/Canada/Australia): Clinical → Bachelor's → Doctorate (PsyD/DClinPsy) → licensure. 6–8 years. Licensing at Master's/Doctoral level. More regulated, higher-paying.

6. IPMAT & Management Option

What Is IPM?

IIM Indore's 5-year IPM: 3 years foundation (economics, stats, humanities, management) + 2 years MBA. Graduate with BBA+MBA from an IIM.
IIM Rohtak's IPM: Similar, includes Logical Reasoning in entrance.
Others: Nirma, TAPMI, IIFT also offer IPM programs.

Is It Realistic to Prepare for Both?

FactorOverlap RealityVerdict
Quantitative Aptitude60–70% overlap between CUET GT and IPMAT Quant. IPMAT is harder.Manageable if Class 10 math is comfortable.
Logical Reasoning70–80% overlap with CUET GT LR.Efficient shared preparation.
Verbal Ability / EnglishBoth require strong English. Common reading/vocab prep works.Shared preparation is efficient.
Psychology & SociologyZero overlap with IPMAT.This is the bottleneck. Consistent time needed.
General KnowledgeOnly in CUET GT. IPMAT does not test GK.Extra burden on CUET prep.

9. Mindset & Decision-Making Framework

The Truth About Your Confusion

You are not broken. You are not directionless. You are standing at a fork with two genuinely good paths. Most students your age have no clarity at all; you have narrowed it down to two. The confusion is not permanent—it's a signal that you haven't yet gathered enough data to choose. Confusion is just data you haven't collected yet.

📅 The 2-Week Action Plan (Start Tomorrow)

Week 1: Information Gathering (June 1–7, 2026)

  • Day 1: Download NCERT Psychology Class 11 PDF. Read Chapter 1. Write: "What excites me about this subject?"
  • Day 2: Read NCERT Psychology Class 12 Chapter 4 ("Psychological Disorders"). Does it energize or exhaust you?
  • Day 3: Go to IIM Indore website. Download IPM brochure & sample papers. Read about the curriculum.
  • Day 4: Take a free CUET Psychology mock (10 questions). Score yourself.
  • Day 5: Take a free CUET Quantitative Aptitude mock (10 questions). Score yourself.
  • Day 6: Watch: "Day in the Life of a Clinical Psychologist in India" + "Day in the Life of an IIM Indore IPM Student" on YouTube.
  • Day 7: Rest. Write down: "If money and prestige were not a factor, which path would I pick? Why?"

Week 2: Deep Engagement & Decision (June 8–14, 2026)

  • Day 8–9: Read Thinking, Fast and Slow (first 50 pages) or The Man Who Mistook His Wife for a Hat. Does the material pull you in?
  • Day 10: Take a cold IPMAT Indore mock test (full-length, timed). Note Quant score + emotional response.
  • Day 11: Visit University of Hyderabad website. Read about their BA Psychology program. Imagine yourself there.
  • Day 12: Visit FLAME University or Azim Premji University website. Compare fees, culture, curriculum.
  • Day 13: Talk to one person currently studying Psychology at a university (LinkedIn, Reddit r/Indian_Academia, or a senior).
  • Day 14: Final Decision Day. Write: "When I imagine my ideal day at age 25, am I in a clinic, a lab, or a corporate office?" Set a phone reminder for July 31, 2026: "Revisit this decision after IPMAT diagnostic."

💌 A Message to Hold Onto

Beta, suno.

You are sitting for an aptitude test and scoring near-perfect in Psychology. That is not a small thing. That is your brain telling you, loudly and with data, what it was built to do. When a test designed to strip away your biases and your overthinking says "this is where you belong," listen.

But I also understand the pull of IIM. The brand. The certainty. The relief of not having to explain your career choice to relatives. That pull is real, and it is not wrong.

Here is what I will say: you can build a secure, meaningful, well-paying life with Psychology. The mental health industry in India is exploding. Teletherapy has made it possible to work with clients across the world. Schools, corporations, hospitals—every institution is waking up to the need for psychologists. And if you study at a good central university, then go abroad for your Master's, you will have a global career.

But if you choose IPM, you will probably never be a psychologist. That door closes, not because it's impossible, but because the momentum of an IIM MBA pulls you into a different world. So the real question is not "which is better?" but "which loss can I live with?"

You do not need to decide today. You have until October 31, 2026. But you do need to start the 2-week action plan tomorrow.

And whatever you choose, you will be okay. Both paths lead to good lives. One just leads to your life. That's the one I hope you pick.
