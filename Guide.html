Psychology Deep Dive

Nuances of Psychology degrees and the strict licensing reality in India that you must know before committing.

🎓

BA (Hons) Psychology

The standard route. Covers clinical, social, organizational psychology. Excellent foundational degree for all master's pathways in India and abroad.

🔬

BSc Psychology

More rigorous (heavy stats, neuroscience). Usually requires a Science background, but places like Azim Premji offer it to Humanities students via bridge courses.

⚠️

BA Programme

Less rigorous. Combines Psych with Sociology/Economics. Avoid if your strict goal is Clinical Psychology, as Master's programs strongly prefer Honours grads.

⚕️

The Indian Licensing Reality (RCI)

You cannot practice as a Clinical Psychologist after a BA. It is strictly illegal to use the title without Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI) registration.

The IPMAT Dilemma

Should you prepare for IPMAT (IIM Indore) alongside CUET? Visualizing the workload and opportunity cost.

Analysis based on latest IPMAT & CUET GT Syllabi.

The Decision Framework

Preparing for both is feasible but grueling. The ~70% overlap between CUET General Test and IPMAT QA/LR is your saving grace. However, IPMAT math is significantly harder.

✅
Do both if: You can commit 1.5 hours daily purely to higher-level math starting June 2026, alongside 2 hours for NCERT domain reading.
❌
Drop IPMAT if: You despise mathematics or struggle with basic Algebra/Geometry. The opportunity cost is ruining your domain subject prep for a highly selective exam.
Crucial Milestone

By January 2027, you must pick a primary target. You cannot ride two boats during the board exam peak. Decide based on your mock scores at that time.
